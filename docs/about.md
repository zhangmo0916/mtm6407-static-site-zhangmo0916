# About Me

## Hello, I'm Mo!

I'm a creative enthusiast with a passion for photography, visual storytelling, and digital design.

Photography has become one of my favourite ways to explore the world, document meaningful moments, and express my creativity.

## My Photography Journey

I enjoy capturing landscapes, city streets, nature, and memorable moments from my travels.

What I love most about photography is the opportunity to discover beauty in ordinary places. Whether it's the warm light of a sunset, interesting architecture, or a quiet moment in nature, I believe every photograph can tell a unique story.

## My Creative Background

I'm currently studying Interactive Media Design at Algonquin College in Ottawa, Canada.

My studies have helped me develop skills in visual composition, colour, digital design, and storytelling.

I enjoy combining photography with design to create engaging visual experiences.

## Let's Connect

I'm always interested in exploring new creative ideas and sharing my photography.

You can find more of my work on [GitHub](https://github.com/zhangmo0916).
